## What we are building

CardioTwin is a Python research prototype for **forecasting a new atrial fibrillation (AF) episode before it starts**, using historical ECG. It also has a FastAPI service, persisted warning state and a dashboard. This work is about improving forecasting evidence; it does not establish clinical readiness.

Fixed research contract:

- Input: the preceding **600 seconds** of ECG; a decision every **30 seconds**.
- Target: new AF onset **120–600 seconds ahead**, minimum AF episode **30 seconds**, post-AF washout **600 seconds**.
- Runtime inputs must never use future samples, future annotations, onset IDs or pair IDs. These are offline supervision/evaluation only.
- Evaluate complete chronological streams, including rejected forecasts, negative-only people, missed events, known-excluded warnings and unknown-outcome warnings.
- Main false-warning budget: **0.25 assessed false warnings per eligible monitoring hour**. Provisional sensitivity goal: **0.50**. These are research assumptions, not clinical standards.
